Legal dropshipping flow

How an order moves

1Buyer pays

A real customer buys from your store. Payment goes to the platform, not from your pocket.

2Supplier fulfils

The order is forwarded to the supplier, who packs and ships it directly to the buyer.

3Tracked delivery

A courier tracking number is attached so you and the buyer can follow the parcel.

4Profit released

After delivery, sale price minus supplier cost and fees is added to your balance.
